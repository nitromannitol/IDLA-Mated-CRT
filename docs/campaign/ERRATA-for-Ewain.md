# Errata: Internal DLA on mated-CRT maps (Bou-Rabee, Gwynne; arXiv:2211.04891)

Ewain, below are the corrections to the printed version, substantive items first, then minor ones. Each item gives the printed text, what is wrong, and the correction. The two longest arguments are written out in Appendices B (Lemma 3.4) and C (Lemma 3.6); Appendix A has a remark on connectedness. Results are numbered as in the paper. Where several corrections touch the same result, they are combined in one item.

## Substantive

### Section 2

**Limit constant $\kappa_0=2/m_0$.** Affected: Lemmas 3.3, 3.4, 3.5 and 3.7, Theorem 4.1 and the remark after it, Lemma 4.8 (items 1 and 3), and the proofs that use them. *Printed:* the limits carry $m_0$, as in $\tfrac{m_0}{6}$, $m_0x$, $m_0\psi$, $-\tfrac{m_0}{6}$. *Wrong:* with $\Delta G=-\delta$, the occupation density of standard Brownian motion is $2G$, so the printed constant is correct only if $m_0^2=2$, which is false for $\gamma^2\le 0.8$. *Correction:* define $\kappa_0:=2/m_0$ after the display eq:mo and replace $m_0$ by $\kappa_0$ in these statements and their proofs (in proofs, $6/m_0$ becomes $6/\kappa_0$). The symbol $c_0$ is not used for this constant, so that it does not clash with the bold radius constant $\boldsymbol c_0$ of Sections 6 and 7. The time rescaling is unchanged. Proposition 4.2, Sections 5 and 7, and Theorems 1.4 and 1.6 use the constant only qualitatively. Appendix B gives the full argument for the occupation identity of Lemma 3.4 with this coefficient. (C1, C17)

### Section 3

**Quantifier order for random subsequences (Lemmas 3.1, 3.3, 3.5, 3.7; Proposition 4.2; Lemmas 4.6 and 4.8).** *Printed:* a random input sequence is fixed before a high-probability extraction, and some limits are stated before the deterministic sequence is prepared. *Wrong:* the extraction needs the deterministic data fixed first. *Correction:* first thin the deterministic sequence deterministically and fix the spatial radii; then allow measurable random subsubsequences and countable families on the inherited event, and state all conclusions on the prepared sequence. In Lemma 3.7 the identifying inputs are prepared before extraction. (C11)

**Lemma 3.1 and the boundary datum.** *Printed:* the bound $u^\varepsilon\le C\varepsilon^{-1}$, and the Hölder bound on boundary data with constant $C'$ (display eq:holder-boundary-data). *Wrong:* $u^\varepsilon=-\varepsilon^{-2}$ satisfies the printed hypothesis while $\overline u^\varepsilon\to-\infty$; the proof and all uses need $|u^\varepsilon|$; the boundary data in Lemma 3.5 are of order $\varepsilon^{-1}$. *Correction:* $|u^\varepsilon|\le C\varepsilon^{-1}$, and the boundary bound carries $C'\varepsilon^{-1}$, so that $\varepsilon u$ satisfies the hypothesis of Lemma 3.2. (C3, C10)

**Lemma 3.2 (Hölder continuity of $\Delta^\varepsilon$-harmonic functions).** *Printed:* the interior bound has the factor $(\varepsilon\vee|\eta(a)-\eta(b)|)^\xi$ for all $a,b\in\mathbb V^\varepsilon(D)$, and the boundary-data part has one exponent $\xi$ and a constant $C$ depending on the data. *Wrong:* the interior estimate needs the floor $\varepsilon^\beta$ in place of $\varepsilon$; the starting vertex $a$ must lie off $\mathbb V^\varepsilon(\partial D)$; and the boundary exponent must depend on the Hölder exponent $\chi$ of the boundary data, the constant on $C'$. *Correction:* there exist $\xi,\beta\in(0,1)$ and $A>0$, depending on $(\rho,\gamma)$, such that, with polynomially high probability, for every connected $D\subset B_\rho$ and every $\Delta^\varepsilon$-harmonic $f^\varepsilon$, $|f^\varepsilon(a)-f^\varepsilon(b)|\le A\bigl(\sup|f^\varepsilon|\bigr)\bigl(\tfrac{\varepsilon^\beta\vee|\eta(a)-\eta(b)|}{\operatorname{dist}(\eta(a),\partial D)}\bigr)^\xi$ for $a\in\mathbb V^\varepsilon(D)\setminus\mathbb V^\varepsilon(\partial D)$ and $b\in\mathbb V^\varepsilon(D)$. For each $\chi\in(0,1]$ there is $\xi'=\xi'(\rho,\gamma,\chi)\le\chi$, and for each $C'>0$ there is $C=C(\rho,\gamma,\chi,C')$, such that for simply connected $D$ and boundary data obeying $|f^\varepsilon(a)-f^\varepsilon(b)|\le C'(\varepsilon\vee|\eta(a)-\eta(b)|)^\chi$ on $\mathbb V^\varepsilon(\partial D)$, the bound $\max\{C,A\sup|f^\varepsilon|\}(\varepsilon\vee|\eta(a)-\eta(b)|)^{\xi'}$ holds for all $a,b\in\mathbb V^\varepsilon(D)$. (C6)

**Lemma 3.4 (killed occupation identity).** *Printed:* the proof uses an unstopped functional and an interchange of limit and expectation that is not justified. *Wrong:* the argument as printed does not establish the identity. *Correction:* a buffered killed-path argument with convergence of exit times, a second-moment tail estimate, and the clock occupation identity, with coefficient $2$ for the unscaled clock and $\kappa_0=2/m_0$ for the paper's process. The complete argument is Appendix B. (C16)

**Lemma 3.5 (discrete Dirichlet problem).** *Printed:* the Hölder boundary datum $\phi$ is evaluated at the exit-cell representative $\eta(b)$. *Wrong:* representatives of boundary cells can lie outside the disc, and the exit functional is not everywhere continuous. *Correction:* evaluate $\phi(\pi_D(\eta(b)))$, where $\pi_D$ is the nearest-point projection onto the closed disc; the same convention applies to all boundary data. (C13)

**Lemma 3.6 (harmonic convergence).** *Printed:* the Hölder datum $\psi$ on $\overline D$ is evaluated at $\eta$ of the exit vertex without projection, and only pointwise convergence is proved. *Wrong:* as for Lemma 3.5. *Correction:* take continuous datum $\psi\in C(\overline D)$, evaluated after nearest-point projection; add uniform convergence and the polynomial same-walk comparison, with a corrected proof for the unrestricted walk. The complete replacement argument is Appendix C. (C13)

**Lemma 3.7 (normalized exit time).** *Printed:* the limiting PDE, stated in the lemma and again in its proof, uses $m_0$. *Correction:* $\kappa_0$ in place of $m_0$, and the deterministic input sequence is prescribed before extraction. (C1, C11, C17)

### Section 4

**Cluster identity (display eq:cluster-definition, Lemma 4.3, Proposition 7.1).** *Printed:* the identity between the divisible sandpile cluster and the closure of the positive odometer, and its consequences, hold with no restriction on $\varepsilon$. *Wrong:* for $\varepsilon\ge t$ no vertex topples and the cluster is $\{0\}$; equality with the stopped cluster also needs the stopped and unstopped odometers to agree. *Correction:* add $\varepsilon<t$; in the displays eq:cluster-and-lambdat (Section 4) and eq:cluster-contained-in (Section 7) assume that the stopped and unstopped odometers agree. This restriction does not cover the earlier use in Proposition 5.1, whose proof is replaced below. (C9)

**Proposition 4.2 (strict interior gap).** *Printed:* the strict gap display eq:strictly-above-in-interior is inferred from continuity alone. *Wrong:* continuity does not exclude a jump of the harmonic-ball family at the fixed time $t$. *Correction:* insert before that display the following argument. The set of $u$ with $p(u)=\mathbb P[\Lambda_u\neq\bigcup_{q<u}\Lambda_q]>0$ is at most countable, and $p(u)=p(bu)$ by scale invariance (Lemma 2.6), so $\Lambda_t=\bigcup_{u<t}\Lambda_u$ almost surely. The obstacle equation (Theorem 2.3(c)) and the strong minimum principle then give $v_t>0$ on $\Lambda_t\setminus\{0\}$, and compactness gives $s>0$. Statement and display are unchanged; the inner spatial radius may depend on the environment, and the constant is $\kappa_0$. (C11, C17, C20)

**Lemmas 4.6 and 4.7.** *Printed:* the deterministic sequence (and, in Lemma 4.6, the fixed time $t$) is quantified after the almost-sure conclusion. *Wrong:* Borel–Cantelli requires a deterministic thinning that depends on the sequence. *Correction:* place the deterministic sequence (and $t$) before the thinning and the almost-sure conclusion. (C12)

**Lemma 4.8.** Items 1 and 3 carry $\kappa_0$ (see the item on the limit constant). *Printed:* the comparison function has boundary value $m_\varepsilon\phi^\delta(\eta(b))+\delta$. *Wrong:* only the grouping $m_\varepsilon\bigl(\phi^\delta(\eta(b))+\delta\bigr)$ gives the stated limiting boundary value and the intended domination. *Correction:* use that grouping. The limits are taken along the prepared deterministic sequence. The boundary-stability step in its proof (the polynomial boundary data differ from their projected values by $o(m_\varepsilon)$) is a consequence of the Lemma 3.6 correction and is marked (C13). (C1, C11, C13, C14, C17)

### Section 5

**Proposition 5.1 and Lemma 5.2.** *Printed:* the proof applies Lemma 5.2 without checking its hypotheses for the chosen sets, uses the full sandpile cluster where coverage by the strict positivity set is needed, and does not justify the stopping domain. *Wrong:* as stated the argument is incomplete. *Correction:* a direct argument: the surplus $u^\varepsilon\ge c/\varepsilon$ on the cells $O_1$ of $B^-_\delta(\Lambda_t)$ (from the strict gap of Proposition 4.2), a maximum-principle comparison for the mean-value inequality, a Green-kernel lower bound, and the vertexwise proof of Lemma 5.2 on the origin component. The statement is unchanged. (C4, C21)

### Section 6

**Good-annulus event (display eq:good-annulus-event).** *Printed:* the integrand $\log\frac1{|u-v|}+1$. *Wrong:* for $u,v\in A_{r,2r}(z)$ one has $|u-v|<4r$, so the printed event is almost surely empty for $r<e^{1-C}/4$; the step asserting that $\mathbb P[E_r(z)]$ does not depend on $r$ or $z$ is valid only for the scale-invariant integrand. *Correction:* the integrand $\log\frac r{|u-v|}+1$. The change of the minimum to an infimum is a minor item below. (C5)

**Lemmas 6.4 to 6.10: settled fixes.** Lemma 6.4 gives $C=C(\gamma,\boldsymbol c_0)$ with $\boldsymbol c_0=\boldsymbol c_0(\rho,\gamma)$ (item below). Lemma 6.5 gives $C=C(p,\gamma,\boldsymbol c_0)$, with the Weyl comparison replaced by a density bound on $A_{r/3,3r}(z)$ and an average over the circle of radius $3r/2$ (item below). Lemma 6.8 is replaced by a target-scale, short-window good-annulus construction, giving $s_1=s_1(\rho,\gamma)$ and $s_2=1/2$, with $s_2$ chosen before $s_0$; its event is written explicitly and its translation step is corrected (items below). Definition 6.2, Lemma 6.3 and Lemma 6.9 take the corrected starting sites and negative-binomial tail events (item below). Lemmas 6.6, 6.7 and 6.10 are unchanged. (C5, C18, C19, C26 to C31)

**Lemma 6.4 (proof).** *Printed:* the proof applies Lemma 6.6, which is stated for annuli centred at $0$, to the events $E_r(z)$ with $z\neq0$, and takes $K=\lfloor\log_7\varepsilon^{-1/2}\rfloor$. *Wrong:* Lemma 6.6 does not apply to annuli centred at $z$ without a translation step, and the number of radii $r_k\in[\delta,\delta^{1/2}]\cap\{7^{-n}\}$ is of order $\log_7\delta^{-1/2}$, not $\log_7\varepsilon^{-1/2}$. *Correction:* apply the translation of the field used in the proof of Lemma 6.8, $h(z+\cdot)=\widetilde h^{\C}+\phi_z$ modulo additive constants, with $\phi_z(w)=-\gamma\log|z+w|$; this gives the hypotheses of Lemma 6.6 with the same constants. Take $K=\lfloor\log_7\delta^{-1/2}\rfloor$. (C32)

**Lemma 6.5.** *Printed:* Step 1 compares the ratios $L/R$ for $h$ and $h^C$ by bounded Weyl densities, and the constant is $C=C(p)$. *Wrong (consequence of the integrand correction above):* the kernel $\log(r/|u-v|)+1$ is negative for $|u-v|>er$, so bounded densities do not give the multiplicative comparison; the constant also depends on $\gamma$ and $\boldsymbol c_0$. *Correction:* write $d\mu_h=q\,d\mu_{h^C}$ with $a\le q\le b$ and $b/a\le A_\gamma$ on $A_{r/3,3r}(z)$; average the $h^C$ event over the circle of radius $3r/2$ to bound the annular mass; bound the positive part of the kernel. This gives $E_r(z;C)$ for $h$ with $C=A_\gamma C_1\bigl(1+\tfrac{\log 4-1}{1-\log 2}\bigr)$, and the statement records $C=C(p,\gamma,\boldsymbol c_0)$. (C28)

**Lemma 6.8.** *Printed statement:* "$X^{a,\varepsilon}$ exits $\mathcal V^\varepsilon(\overline\Theta+B_r)\setminus\Theta$ through $A$", where $A$ is a subset of the domain rather than of its exit boundary. *Correction:* with $U=\mathcal V^\varepsilon(\overline\Theta+B_r)\setminus\Theta$ and $\tau$ the exit time of $U$, the event is $\{X_n\in A \text{ for every } 0\le n<\tau\}$, as used in the proof and in Section 7. *Printed proof:* it does not establish the lemma: its Step 1 good annuli lie outside the walk's exit domain, Steps 2 and 4 use the non-scale-invariant kernel (a consequence of the integrand correction above), and several smaller gaps remain. *Correction:* the proof is replaced by a target-scale, short-window good-annulus construction with two-sided-shell amplification. The statement is unchanged. The new proof gives $s_1=s_1(\rho,\gamma)$ and $s_2=1/2$, chosen before $s_0$. It also requires the bold radius constant to be smaller than before: in addition to the condition of eq:choice-of-constant-annulus, $\boldsymbol c_0<1/48$ and $C_{\rm G}^{-1}\log\frac1{8\boldsymbol c_0}-1\geq\frac12$ (display eq:c26-choice-c0). The proof also writes $m_0:=\mu_h(D_0)$ locally; this is a local notation, not the median $m_0$ of eq:mo. (C26, C30)

**Definition 6.2, Lemma 6.3 and Lemma 6.9.** *Printed:* the starting sites $a_1,\dots,a_k$ lie in $\Theta$ (Definition 6.2, Lemma 6.3 "subset $\Theta$", and the proof of Lemma 6.9), while Lemma 6.9 is stated for $a_i\in\overline\Theta$. *Wrong:* restarted aggregates start walkers on the boundary of the stopping set, so the printed inclusions are inconsistent with their use. *Correction:* $a_i\in\Theta'$ in Definition 6.2 and Lemma 6.3 (a walker started at an unoccupied vertex is absorbed there at time zero), and $a_i\in\overline\Theta$ in the proof of Lemma 6.9. In the same proof, the events in the two negative-binomial bounds are reversed (a typo): the correct forms are $\mathbb P[\mathcal N(k_1)>(s_2^{-1}+C)k_1\mid h,\eta]\le e^{-ck_1}$ and $\mathbb P[\mathcal N((s_2^{-1}+C)^{-1}k)>k\mid h,\eta]\le e^{-ck}$. (C19, C31)

### Section 7

**Lemma 7.2 and Proposition 7.1.** *Printed:* Lemma 7.2 is stated for all $s>0$, and Proposition 7.1 applies it without a lower scale. *Wrong:* the estimate fails below the cell scale. *Correction:* replace $s>0$ by $s\ge\varepsilon^\beta$ for a deterministic $\beta>0$, and restrict the application in Proposition 7.1 accordingly. (C8)

**Lemma 7.4 and Step 3 of Proposition 7.1.** *Printed:* Lemma 7.4 starts from every $a\in\mathbb D_\varepsilon(t\varepsilon^{-1})$ and requires the path to stay in that cluster, and the words "for all $t>0$" lie outside "with polynomially high probability". *Wrong:* $\mathbb D_\varepsilon(t\varepsilon^{-1})$ is the closure of the positive stopped support, so a starting point may have zero stopped odometer, and the first increment (by $(\log\varepsilon^{-1})^{-2}$) need not hold. *Correction:* on one degree-bound event, simultaneously for all $t>0$ and all nonzero $a\in\Lambda_t^\varepsilon=\{u_t^\varepsilon>0\}$, construct the increasing path inside $\Lambda_t^\varepsilon$; the boundary-start alternative is deleted. In Step 3 of Proposition 7.1, apply the path to each $a\in\Lambda_t^\varepsilon$ outside $\Theta_{\tau_{\varepsilon,2}}$ and stop it on first reaching the boundary of $\Theta_{\tau_{\varepsilon,2}}$; the path bound reads "the odometer increases by at most $\varepsilon^{-\omega_0}$". (C25)

## Minor

**Median in eq:mo (Section 2).** *Printed:* the median of the unscaled Liouville Brownian motion exit time from $B_{1/2}$ starts from a free point $z$. *Correction:* the start point is $0$, as in the discrete median eq:meps. (C15)

**Lemmas 5.3 and 5.4.** *Printed:* the separation condition is $\operatorname{dist}(\eta(a),\partial\eta(\Lambda^\varepsilon_t))>\delta$. *Wrong:* $\eta(\Lambda^\varepsilon_t)$ is a finite point set, so the condition never holds and the lemmas are vacuous. *Correction:* $\partial\overline{\Lambda^\varepsilon_t}$, the boundary of the closure of the cell union. In Lemma 5.3 the surplus variable is written $\mathfrak s=\mathfrak s(\delta,t)$, matching the order of the quantifiers (a clarification, not an error). The same boundary predicate replaces the four occurrences in the proof of Proposition 5.1. (C4, C23)

**Proposition 5.1 (boundary vanishing).** *Printed:* the ambient Green function $\gr{\varepsilon}{B_\rho}(\cdot,0)$ and $\tilde w$ are said to vanish individually on $\partial\Lambda^\varepsilon_t$. *Wrong:* only $\tilde w+(t\varepsilon^{-1})\gr{\varepsilon}{B_\rho}(\cdot,0)$ vanishes there, by obstacle contact; $\gr{\varepsilon}{\Lambda^\varepsilon_t}$ and $\qeps{\Lambda^\varepsilon_t}$ vanish by killing. *Correction:* state this in the proof; the statement is unchanged. (C34)

**Lemma 6.4 (constant).** *Printed:* the constant $C$ depends only on $\gamma$. *Correction:* $C$ depends on $\gamma$ and $\boldsymbol c_0$, with $\boldsymbol c_0=\boldsymbol c_0(\rho,\gamma)$; all later uses fix $\rho$. A good annulus forces $C\ge(1-\log2)/(16\boldsymbol c_0^2)$, so dependence on $\gamma$ alone is not justified (the statement fails if $\inf_\rho \boldsymbol c_0=0$). The stale constant $C(\gamma)$ in the printed proof is corrected too. (C18)

**Lemma 6.9 (quantifier order).** *Printed:* $\omega$ and $s_3$ are chosen before $s_0$. *Correction:* $s_3>0$ is chosen after $s_0$, together with $s_4,s_5$, as in Lemma 6.7, whose success probability may depend on $s_0$; the proof is unchanged. (C35)

**Lemma 7.3 (clarification).** *Printed:* the comparison has no stated probabilistic scope. *Correction:* it holds almost surely, simultaneously for every $\varepsilon>0$. The quantifiers over $t$, $B$ and $b$, the displayed inequality and the proof are unchanged. (C24)

**Appendix A (connectedness).** *Printed:* Appendix A assumes that the finite set $V$ is connected. *Correction:* $G$ is assumed connected (and locally finite and infinite), and $V$ is any finite set containing the origin. The proofs are componentwise, as the remark in Appendix A explains. This covers the Section 4 reduction, in which the ball-interior set may be disconnected. (C33)

**Normalisation of the Green's function.** *Printed:* in the preliminaries and in Section 3 the Green's function is called the Green's function for standard Brownian motion. *Correction:* state the normalisation $\Delta G=-\delta$, under which the occupation density of standard Brownian motion is $2G$, as in the definition of $\kappa_0$. (C1)

**Maximum principle, Lemma 2.13.** *Printed:* "for every connected subset $A\subset\mathbb V^\varepsilon$". *Wrong:* it fails for $A=\mathbb V^\varepsilon$ and $u\equiv 1$, since then $A$ has empty boundary. *Correction:* "for every nonempty finite subset $A$". Connectedness is not needed (apply the statement to each component), and all uses are finite sets. (C2)

**Lemmas 4.4 and 4.5.** *Printed:* the origin clause of Lemma 4.4 and the mass identity of Lemma 4.5 hold for all $\varepsilon>0$. *Wrong:* if the source cell meets $\partial B_\rho$, the killed source and the mass sum can vanish. *Correction:* assume $0\in\mathbb V^\varepsilon(B_\rho)\setminus\mathbb V^\varepsilon(\partial B_\rho)$; this holds for all sufficiently small $\varepsilon$ by continuity of $\eta$ at $0$. (C7)

**Good-annulus event: minimum replaced by infimum.** *Printed:* a minimum over the open annulus $A_{r,2r}(z)$ of $\mu_h(B_{\boldsymbol c_0r}(w))$ in eq:good-annulus-event. *Wrong:* the annulus is open, so the minimum need not be attained; the proof of Lemma 6.8 already uses an infimum. *Correction:* an infimum, which is positive by a finite net and full support of $\mu_h$. (C27)
