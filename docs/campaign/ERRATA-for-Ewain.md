# Errata: Internal DLA on mated-CRT maps (Bou-Rabee, Gwynne; arXiv:2211.04891)

Ewain, below are the corrections to the printed version, substantive items first, then minor ones. Each item gives the printed text, what is wrong, and the correction. The two longest arguments are written out in Appendices B (Lemma 3.4) and C (Lemma 3.6); Appendix A has a remark on connectedness. Results are numbered as in the paper. Where several corrections touch the same result, they are combined in one item.

## Substantive

### Section 2

**Limit constant $\lambda_0=2/m_0$.** Affected: Lemmas 3.3, 3.4, 3.5 and 3.7, Theorem 4.1 and the remark after it, Lemma 4.8 (items 1 and 3), and the proofs that use them. *Printed:* the limits carry $m_0$, as in $\tfrac{m_0}{6}$, $m_0x$, $m_0\psi$, $-\tfrac{m_0}{6}$. *Wrong:* with $\Delta G=-\delta$, the occupation density of standard Brownian motion is $2G$, so the printed constant is correct only if $m_0^2=2$, which is false for $\gamma^2\le 0.8$. *Correction:* define $\lambda_0:=2/m_0$ after the display eq:mo and replace $m_0$ by $\lambda_0$ in these statements and their proofs (in proofs, $6/m_0$ becomes $6/\lambda_0$). The symbol $c_0$ is not used for this constant, so that it does not clash with the bold radius constant $\boldsymbol c_0$ of Sections 6 and 7. The time rescaling is unchanged. Proposition 4.2, Sections 5 and 7, and Theorems 1.4 and 1.6 use the constant only qualitatively. Appendix B gives the full argument for the occupation identity of Lemma 3.4 with this coefficient. (C1, C17)

### Section 3

**Quantifier order for random subsequences (Lemmas 3.1, 3.3, 3.5, 3.7; the proofs of Lemma 3.4 and Theorem 4.1; Proposition 4.2; Lemmas 4.6 and 4.8).** *Printed:* a random input sequence is fixed before a high-probability extraction, and some limits are stated before the deterministic sequence is prepared. *Wrong:* the extraction needs the deterministic data fixed first. *Correction:* first thin the deterministic sequence deterministically and fix the spatial radii; then allow measurable random subsubsequences and countable families on the inherited event, and state all conclusions on the prepared sequence. In Lemma 3.7 the identifying inputs are prepared before extraction. (C11)

**Lemma 3.1 and the boundary datum.** *Printed:* the bound $u^\varepsilon\le C\varepsilon^{-1}$, and the Hölder bound on boundary data with constant $C'$ (display eq:holder-boundary-data). *Wrong:* $u^\varepsilon=-\varepsilon^{-2}$ satisfies the printed hypothesis while $\overline u^\varepsilon\to-\infty$; the proof and all uses need $|u^\varepsilon|$; the boundary data in Lemma 3.5 are of order $\varepsilon^{-1}$. *Correction:* $|u^\varepsilon|\le C\varepsilon^{-1}$, and the boundary bound carries $C'\varepsilon^{-1}$, so that $\varepsilon u$ satisfies the hypothesis of Lemma 3.2. (C3, C10)

**Lemma 3.2 (Hölder continuity of $\Delta^\varepsilon$-harmonic functions).** *Printed:* the interior bound has the factor $(\varepsilon\vee|\eta(a)-\eta(b)|)^\xi$ for all $a,b\in\mathbb V^\varepsilon(D)$, and the boundary-data part has one exponent $\xi$ and a constant $C$ depending on the data. *Wrong:* the interior estimate needs the floor $\varepsilon^\beta$ in place of $\varepsilon$; the starting vertex $a$ must lie off $\mathbb V^\varepsilon(\partial D)$; and the boundary exponent must depend on the Hölder exponent $\chi$ of the boundary data, the constant on $C'$. *Correction:* there exist $\xi,\beta\in(0,1)$ and $A>0$, depending on $(\rho,\gamma)$, such that, with polynomially high probability, for every connected $D\subset B_\rho$ and every $\Delta^\varepsilon$-harmonic $f^\varepsilon$, $|f^\varepsilon(a)-f^\varepsilon(b)|\le A\bigl(\sup|f^\varepsilon|\bigr)\bigl(\tfrac{\varepsilon^\beta\vee|\eta(a)-\eta(b)|}{\operatorname{dist}(\eta(a),\partial D)}\bigr)^\xi$ for $a\in\mathbb V^\varepsilon(D)\setminus\mathbb V^\varepsilon(\partial D)$ and $b\in\mathbb V^\varepsilon(D)$. For each $\chi\in(0,1]$ there is $\xi'=\xi'(\rho,\gamma,\chi)\le\chi$, and for each $C'>0$ there is $C=C(\rho,\gamma,\chi,C')$, such that for simply connected $D$ and boundary data obeying $|f^\varepsilon(a)-f^\varepsilon(b)|\le C'(\varepsilon\vee|\eta(a)-\eta(b)|)^\chi$ on $\mathbb V^\varepsilon(\partial D)$, the bound $\max\{C,A\sup|f^\varepsilon|\}(\varepsilon\vee|\eta(a)-\eta(b)|)^{\xi'}$ holds for all $a,b\in\mathbb V^\varepsilon(D)$. (C6)

**Lemma 3.4 (killed occupation identity).** *Printed:* the proof uses an unstopped functional and an interchange of limit and expectation that is not justified. *Wrong:* the argument as printed does not establish the identity. *Correction:* a buffered killed-path argument with convergence of exit times, a second-moment tail estimate, and the clock occupation identity, with coefficient $2$ for the unscaled clock and $\lambda_0=2/m_0$ for the paper's process. The complete argument is Appendix B. *Coefficient for $X$:* for $\gamma=1/2$, $R=1/2$ and $L=\tau^{Y,0}_{B_R}$ (the exit time of $B_R$ by the unscaled process $Y$ from $0$), $\mathbb E L=2R^{2-\gamma^2}/(2-\gamma^2)^2<0.195$, so $\mathbb P(L\ge1)<1/2$ and every median $m_0$ is less than $1$. The positive Green integral in the scaled identity then rules out the coefficient $2$ for $X$. The minimal coefficient correction is $\lambda_0=2/m_0$; coefficient $2$ is correct for the raw process $Y$. (C16)

**Lemma 3.5 (discrete Dirichlet problem).** *Printed:* the Hölder boundary datum $\phi$ is evaluated at the exit-cell representative $\eta(b)$. *Wrong:* representatives of boundary cells can lie outside the disc, and the exit functional is not everywhere continuous. *Correction:* evaluate $\phi(\pi_D(\eta(b)))$, where $\pi_D$ is the nearest-point projection onto the closed disc; the same convention applies to all boundary data. (C13)

**Lemma 3.6 (harmonic convergence).** *Printed:* the Hölder datum $\psi$ on $\overline D$ is evaluated at $\eta$ of the exit vertex without projection, and only pointwise convergence is proved. *Wrong:* as for Lemma 3.5. The printed proof has three further defects: (i) a boundary cell need not have its representative $\eta(a)$ in $\overline D$, so $\psi(\eta(X^\eps_{\sigma^\eps_D}))$ is undefined if $\psi$ is specified only on $\overline D$; (ii) the exit functional is not an everywhere-defined continuous functional on the printed path space (a constant path in $D$ never exits, and uniform convergence need not preserve exit locations at a path that touches the boundary and returns without crossing); (iii) the walk $\widehat X^{y,\eps}$ there is stopped on boundary cells of $B_\rho$, so for $D=B_\rho$ it can be frozen at a representative inside $D$ and its Euclidean exit time can be infinite. The last display of the printed proof also mixes the notation for LBM and the discrete walk. *Correction:* take continuous datum $\psi\in C(\overline D)$, evaluated after nearest-point projection; add uniform convergence. The polynomial same-walk comparison is an optional strengthening, not used by any other result of the paper, and it is proved for the unrestricted walk. The complete replacement argument is Appendix C. (C13)

**Lemma 3.7 (normalized exit time).** *Printed:* the limiting PDE, stated in the lemma and again in its proof, uses $m_0$. *Correction:* $\lambda_0$ in place of $m_0$, and the deterministic input sequence is prescribed before extraction. (C1, C11, C17)

## Editorial and citation notes (not errata)

These items are omitted justifications in the printed proof of Proposition 7.1. None changes a statement. The replacement in the Section 7 entry above repairs each of them.

**Proposition 7.1, D1 (the bound $\varepsilon O_0\to0$).** The printed bound $O_0<\boldsymbol\delta_0\varepsilon^{-1}$ alone does not give $r_1\le r_0=\boldsymbol\delta_0$. The convergence $\varepsilon O_0\to0$ gives $r_1\le\boldsymbol\delta_0$ with probability tending to one, for fixed $\boldsymbol\delta_0$. This should be written out (T4).

**Proposition 7.1, D3 (connectedness).** Lemma 7.2 needs a connected set containing $0$, and $\Theta_{k-1}$ need not be connected. It is applied to the component $T$ of $\Theta_{k-1}$ containing $0$; the fact $\partial T\subset\partial\Theta_{k-1}$, which gives $\sup_{\partial T}u_t^\varepsilon\le O_{k-1}$, should be stated (T5).

**Proposition 7.1, D5 (exponent).** From $O_{k-1}\le(1-s_2)^{k-1}O_0$ the exponent is $(1-s_2)^{k-1}$, not $(1-s_2)^k$ (T7). The extra slack $\varepsilon O_0\le(1-s_2)\boldsymbol\delta_0$ holds with probability tending to one.

**Proposition 7.1, D6 (cell size at the boundary).** The bound at tex :4083 controls $u_t^\varepsilon$ on $\mathcal V^\varepsilon(\partial\Lambda_t)$, whereas $O_0$ is a maximum over the graph boundary $\partial\Theta_0$ of $\Theta_0=\mathcal V^\varepsilon(\Lambda_t)$. The cells of boundary vertices lie within $2d_\varepsilon$ of $\partial\Lambda_t$ (T3).

**Proposition 7.1, D7 (localization and bookkeeping).** The localization, the first-exit bootstrap and the mesh bookkeeping are omitted from the printed proof (T1, T9). The random parameter $\boldsymbol\delta$ of Lemma 6.8 is allowed as it stands; localizing it is optional, and T1 makes it deterministic in the replacement.
